What We Do

Our Services

From a loose button to a full garment overhaul — we handle it all with precision and care.

✂️

Alterations & Tailoring

Perfect fit adjustments for pants, dresses, shirts, and suits. We take in, let out, shorten, or lengthen any garment.

  • Hem trousers & skirts
  • Take in / let out seams
  • Sleeve shortening
  • Waist adjustments
🧵

Seam & Tear Repairs

Ripped seams, split linings, torn pockets — we restore structural integrity so your garment looks like new.

  • Seam re-stitching
  • Lining replacement
  • Pocket repair & replacement
  • Tear & hole mending
🔒

Zipper Replacement

Broken, stuck, or missing zippers on jackets, dresses, bags, and boots — replaced quickly and reliably.

  • Jacket & coat zippers
  • Dress & skirt zippers
  • Boot & shoe zippers
  • Bag & luggage zippers
🔘

Buttons & Fasteners

Lost or damaged buttons replaced to match. We stock hundreds of styles or can source a custom match for you.

  • Button replacement
  • Snap & hook installation
  • Buttonhole repair
  • Decorative button sourcing
🩹

Patching & Darning

Invisible mending and decorative patching for denim, knitwear, wool, and more. We preserve the garment's character.

  • Denim patches
  • Elbow & knee patches
  • Knitwear darning
  • Moth hole repair
👗

Special Occasion

Wedding dresses, formal wear, and heirloom garments handled with the utmost care and attention to detail.

  • Bridal alterations
  • Suit tailoring
  • Vintage restoration
  • Heirloom preservation

Our Story

A Family Craft,
A Community Service

What started as a small table in our living room has grown into the neighborhood's most trusted repair shop. For over 25 years, we've been mending, altering, and transforming garments for thousands of families in this community.

Every piece that comes through our door is treated with the same care as if it were our own. We believe clothes carry memories — and we're here to help keep those memories alive.
